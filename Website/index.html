Our Menu

Every Dish, a Family Tradition

From our signature birria de res to our famous quesabirria, every item is crafted with slow-braised care and generations of flavor.

Birria de Res

Our signature dish — tender beef slow-braised for 12 hours in a rich blend of dried chiles, spices, and our secret family adobo. Served with handmade tortillas and consomé.

Birria Tacos

Crispy corn tortillas dipped in consomé, filled with juicy shredded birria, topped with cilantro and onion. Served with a side of rich consomé for dipping.

Quesabirria

The fan favorite — melted Oaxaca cheese and tender birria folded into a crispy, consomé-dipped tortilla. Golden, cheesy, and impossibly satisfying.

Birria Ramen

East meets West — ramen noodles swimming in our deeply flavorful birria consomé, topped with tender braised beef, cilantro, lime, and a soft-boiled egg.

Mulitas & Consomé

Thick, handmade tortillas pressed with birria and melted cheese, griddled until golden. Paired with our legendary consomé — the soul of every birria dish.

Catering Services

Bring the Familia Castro experience to your next event. Full birria catering for parties, weddings, corporate events, and celebrations of all sizes.

Our Story

A Legacy Built on Flavor

Generational Roots

The Castro family recipe was born in Jalisco, Mexico — passed down through three generations. Every pot of birria carries the soul of our abuela's kitchen.

Slow-Cooked Craft

We start before sunrise. Our beef is marinated overnight in a complex blend of dried guajillo, ancho, and árbol chiles, then slow-braised for 12 hours until fork-tender.

Community First

From our spot on Vineland Ave to your family's table, we serve North Hollywood and the San Fernando Valley with pride, love, and the best birria in LA.
